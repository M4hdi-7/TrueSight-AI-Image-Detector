## 8.1 How to format entries

This dissertation uses **IEEE-style numbered references**. Cite in the body of the text using bracketed numbers (`[1]`, `[2]`) corresponding to the entries below. Order the entries in the order of first appearance in the text, not alphabetically.

The structure of each entry depends on its type:

- **Web page / online documentation**: Author / Organization, "Title," *Site name*, Date. [Online]. Available: URL. [Accessed: DD-Mon-YYYY].
- **Journal paper**: Author(s), "Title," *Journal name*, vol. X, no. Y, pp. PP–PP, Month Year.
- **Conference paper**: Author(s), "Title," in *Proceedings name*, City, Year, pp. PP–PP.
- **Book**: Author, *Title*, edition. City: Publisher, Year.
- **Standard / specification**: Organization, "Title," Standard number, Year. [Online]. Available: URL.
- **News article**: Reporter, "Headline," *Publication*, Date. [Online]. Available: URL.
